3 · Safe harbor — dodge the penalty

If you underpay during the year, the IRS charges a penalty. The safe harbor: pay at least 100% of last year's tax in quarterly installments (110% if last year's AGI topped $150k) and you owe no penalty even if this year's estimate is low.
